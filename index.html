About

A bit about how I got here

I'm a final-year B.Sc. Information Technology (Data Science) student at Stamford International University in Bangkok, currently holding a 4.0 GPA. Before that, I completed a UK-accredited BTEC HND in Software Engineering at Gusto College in Yangon, graduating with all distinctions.

My background is a mix of hands-on IT support and hands-on software work — I spent a year keeping network infrastructure and internal tools running for a 38-branch organization before shifting fully into development. That experience left me with a strong instinct for building things that are dependable in production, not just in a demo.

These days I build backend APIs with FastAPI, work with relational and vector databases, and build applied ML and RAG pipelines using LangChain and Ollama. I enjoy the part of engineering where data, infrastructure, and product decisions meet.
